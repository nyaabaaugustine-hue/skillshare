# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Learners aged 8–17 using a phone or browser to learn Python, understand errors,
and practise at their own pace. Parents and guardians use the wider app to
follow learning progress.

## Product Purpose

SkillsBridge Academy helps young learners build practical Python skills with
guided lessons, an accessible code editor, and a tutor that remains useful
online and offline. The tutor should explain before linking out, personalize
practice to learner progress, and offer safe visual and audio learning aids.

## Brand Personality

Encouraging, imaginative, and trustworthy. The experience should feel polished
and calm for children without being patronizing, with clear teaching and
expressive visual explanations.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic chatbot templates, clutter, childish or condescending visuals,
distracting motion, and unsafe or unsourced content. Do not send guests to
remote AI services.

## Design Principles

- Teach the idea before recommending an external resource.
- Let learners choose between concise text, accessible visuals, and optional
  speech.
- Personalize practice using only the active learner's minimum necessary
  progress data.
- Keep interactions calm, legible, and encouraging at every screen size.
- Preserve useful offline behavior whenever network services fail.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, scalable text, reduced-motion support, screen-reader
labels, keyboard accessibility, and captions or transcripts for audio/video.
